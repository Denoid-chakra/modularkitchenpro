About Us

Delhi’s Most Trusted
Kitchen Specialists

For over 18 years, Modular Kitchen has been transforming homes across Dwarka and Delhi NCR. We design, manufacture and install modular kitchens that are built around how Indian families actually cook — with space for the kadhai, the pressure cooker, the masala dabbas, and everything in between.

Every kitchen is designed from scratch, built in our own workshop, and installed by our trained team. No middlemen. No compromise.

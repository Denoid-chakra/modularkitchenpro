Our Services

Everything you need — under one roof.

What We Do

End-to-End Kitchen Solutions

From the first sketch to the final screw — we handle every step in-house so you never have to coordinate with multiple vendors.

◯

Kitchen Design

Our designers visit your home, study your space and cooking habits, and create a custom kitchen layout tailored to you. We design for Indian kitchens — proper space for the tadka, the wet prep, and the storage of a hundred spice jars.

△

Manufacturing

Every cabinet is made in our own Dwarka workshop using BWR Marine Plywood, precision CNC cutting and premium edge banding. We control quality at every step — no outsourcing, no shortcuts.

◆

Installation

Our trained installation team handles delivery and fitting. We protect your floors and walls during installation, complete the job in 7–12 days (depending on size), and leave your kitchen spotless.

▭

After-Sales Support

A kitchen is a long-term investment. Our dedicated after-sales team is always available for any adjustments, hardware replacements, or issues that arise after installation.

▵

Renovation & Upgrade

Already have a kitchen but want to upgrade the shutters, add new units, or change the countertop? We handle kitchen renovations too — minimal disruption, maximum upgrade.

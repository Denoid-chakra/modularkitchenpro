Our Story

18 years. 564 kitchens. One passion.

Who We Are

Born in Dwarka.
Built for India.

Modular Kitchen was founded in Dwarka, Delhi in 2006 with a simple belief: every Indian family deserves a kitchen that is beautiful, functional, and built to last. We started with a small workshop and a team of four craftsmen. Today, we have a state-of-the-art manufacturing facility, a team of 30+ skilled professionals, and over 564 completed projects across Delhi NCR.

We design every kitchen from scratch — no catalogue copy-paste. Our designers sit with you, understand your cooking habits, your storage needs, your aesthetic preferences — and then create a kitchen that is uniquely yours.
